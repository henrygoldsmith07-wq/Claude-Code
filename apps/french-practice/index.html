Le Studio — French Speaking Practice

A free, all-in-one studio for practising French — speaking, listening, reading and writing — with an AI conversation partner. Everything runs in your browser: no backend, no account, and your progress stays on your device.

What's inside

  • Roleplay Arena — voice conversations across 14 scenarios (bistro, flight rebooking, market, bank, doctor, hairdresser, flat rental, job interview, free talk…) with per-turn corrections and scored feedback.
  • Learning Path — goal-based roadmaps (travel, school, business, fluency): 12 units and 60 lessons per goal, a CEFR placement test (A1–C2), scored checkpoints and adaptive level tracking.
  • Knowledge stats — a knowledge score, 8-week growth chart, and tiles for words known, grammar mastered, lessons done and CEFR level.
  • Vocabulary — 18 themed flashcard packs with SM-2 spaced repetition and a ~2,000-word frequency-ranked offline dictionary with IPA and text-to-speech.
  • Skills — pronunciation coaching, minimal pairs, dictée, mini-podcasts, dialogues, graded readers, articles, classics and writing drills.
  • Grammar — 8 interactive CEFR-tagged topics with drills, sentence building and scored quizzes, plus conjugation tables.
  • Culture — customs, etiquette, festivals, food, history, geography and regional differences.
  • Motivation — XP, streaks, goals, achievements, habit tracker, focus timer, heatmaps and weekly reports. Installable PWA, works offline.
