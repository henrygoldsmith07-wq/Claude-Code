FAQ

Is this impartial AI news?

No — the headline is deliberately not that. It is: what happened, where, who is reporting and where they differ.

What keys do I need?

GEMINI_API_KEY for grounded summaries; optional GROQ/OPENROUTER/SUPABASE for extras.

Can I pre-cache?

With Supabase and CRON_SECRET, /api/cron/refresh warms world + major countries daily.
