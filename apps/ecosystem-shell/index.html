Every app below is served from this one origin, which is what lets Pulse read what they record without any of it leaving your browser.

  • Pulse Evidence about you, with its working shown
  • Arise Training sessions, programs and daily readiness
  • Reflect Structured reflection with a longitudinal review
  • Revise Spaced repetition on an FSRS schedule
  • French Practice Conversation, pronunciation and review
  • Rapport Conversation drills and challenges

Pulse reads a source only once you switch it on in the app that owns the data, and it reads the one storage key that app writes — nothing else.
