Transcription

Cheap to run

FAQ

What do I configure?

web/.env.local: MONGODB_URI/DB, R2_* keys, GROQ_API_KEY, ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, DESKTOP_API_KEY.

CORS?

Bucket must allow PUT/GET for presigned uploads — paste r2-cors.json in R2 Settings or via wrangler.

Public share?

Yes — public share links when you want them; otherwise presigned private playback.
