FAQ

What key?

GROQ_API_KEY from console.groq.com — free, near-instant Whisper.

Hotkey format?

+-separated: modifiers ctrl/alt/shift/win + optional key (space, f8, letter).

Why paste mode?

It handles any Unicode and works across more apps than synthetic keystrokes.
